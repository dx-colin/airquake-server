Map: BJAIR1.BSP

Author: Bjoern Drabeck (drabeck@netway.at)

Description: Rather small level best suited for deathmatches. I have 
tried to build it so that ground vehicles also have a chance to get 
some frags ;-). So there are some places where you can hide, but there
are no places where a skilled pilot can't go (fly) to. For your 
information: Aircraft can land on all roads (even in the tunnel) and if
you are a skillful pilot you can also land in the cave on the hill
(near the "town"), this works best with the helo, the A10 and the X29.
Repair points are in the hangars and weapons are all around the level.
The helo can also land on top of the buildings in the city, but just 
on the "pads" (the places where you find goodies).
You can also play this level in single player, but I did not put very
much effort in placing the vehicles, so I think they will not behave
really "intelligent" - but try it anyway.
