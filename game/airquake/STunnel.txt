Title                   : Speed Tunnel (Official Winner of The Hangers "CRAZY Map Award"!!!!)
Filename                : Stunnel
Author                  : Hanh "Wanderer" Court
Email Address           : EtherealHC@aol.com
Description             : A simple map in which you try to make a 
			  mad dash through it without crashing.
			  It is possible to make it all the way through
			  without braking of any sort (ie:gears or speedbrakes)
			  and keeping the throttle at 100% (Not afterburners)
			  
			  DM'ing is recommend, otherwise it gets boring.
			  
			  For use only with planes in AirQuake2
			  
			  Thanks to Pepsi Lizard who mentioned something
			  like this on the webboard. Sorry I'm horrible
			  with names.

			  Fixed a bug in which one of the guys started sideways.
			  Maybe later I'll make those cubes at the top into real lights.
			  Only works with Airquake2 ver 0.30

			  WARNING: There are a couple of doors, the one near the beginning you
			  just shoot and it opens the other opens on it's own... or it should.
