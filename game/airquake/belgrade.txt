* Notes from the author *

My first map released, finally.
Worked for a long time on it, it should be
done by now, I hope there are no problems
If you have low video ram you
probably get a disappearing building or two
in the city, I get that too, I really pushed
the limit with the number of faces at a time.
To get rid of the disappearing entities, type
r_maxsurfs 2500 and r_maxedges 5000 in your
console. That should stop them from disappearing.
Hopefully in the Quake 2 version I can make
the ship move around and maybe add more
buildings on the NATO side. Oh and you can destroy
about almost everything except the streets,
the lights and the fences. Enjoy!
