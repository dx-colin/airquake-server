Title                   : Sea Strike
Date                    : 8/7/99
Filename                : seastrik.bsp  
Author(s)               : Brian Rivers
Email Address           : brivers@cc.gatech.edu

Description             : A new level for AirQuake 1. It's a 
                          water-based level divided into two
                          bases which are separated by water and
                          a big wall. It's a really tall level, 
                          so there's plenty of room for dogfights 
                          and stuff. 

                          Ground vehicles are supported, and can
                          travel between the two bases by a neat 
                          little landing craft.

                          Single player and Teamplay DM are kinda 
                          tough because of the defense arrays 
                          around both bases.
